§2.12 · The switch-over

Nigeria's own IPSAS timeline

Individual standards each have their own IPSASB issue date, but the one date that actually gets examined is when Nigeria itself moved onto them — and that happened in exactly two steps, on the recommendation of the Federation Accounts Allocation Committee (FAAC).

2014
Cash Basis IPSAS adopted

Financial Reporting Under the Cash Basis of Accounting — the standalone (unnumbered) cash-basis standard — takes effect from 1 January 2014. Covered fully in Chapter 14.

2016
Accrual Basis IPSAS adopted

Nigeria moves to the full suite of numbered accrual-basis standards from 1 January 2016, using the transitional relief in IPSAS 33 (Chapter 15) to phase recognition/measurement in over up to three years rather than restating everything at once.

Frequently tested, easy to swap

Cash basis = 2014; accrual basis = 2016. An exam option often offers both years for both bases, banking on the swap.

Two supersessions worth knowing both numbers for

IPSAS 17 → IPSAS 45 (Property, Plant and Equipment) and IPSAS 25 → IPSAS 39 (Employee Benefits). Older past questions and the study text's own transitional-relief wording still cite the retired number; a current question is more likely to use the replacement. Learn the pair, not just one half of it.
