Ch 1 · International standards & professional bodies

1 · International standards bodies

IASC
International Accounting Standards Committee

Formed 1973 — the first international standard-setter; reorganised in 2001 into the IASB.

IASB
International Accounting Standards Board

The IASC's successor — the independent board that sets international (private-sector) accounting standards.

IFAC
International Federation of Accountants

Global body for the accountancy profession, founded 1977 — 173 members/associates across 129 countries. Issues IPSAS through its own board, the IPSASB.

IPSASB
International Public Sector Accounting Standards Board

IFAC's standard-setting board for the public sector (formerly the Public Sector Committee, PSC) — 18 members, 15 nominated by IFAC member bodies plus 3 public members.

IPSAS
International Public Sector Accounting Standards

The standards themselves — high-quality global standards for public-sector entities, other than Government Business Enterprises (GBEs). See the full IPSAS index.

AICPA
American Institute of Certified Public Accountants

US professional body — historically recommended GAAP-based financial statements for governmental units.

INTOSAI
International Organisation of Supreme Audit Institutions

Worldwide association of every country's Auditor-General equivalent — sets audit-practice resolutions and standards.

PAFA
Pan African Federation of Accountants

Continental accountancy body, established May 2011 — drives the development of Africa's accountancy profession in support of AU Agenda 2063.

API
African Professionalisation Initiative

Partnership of Accountants-General, professional accountancy bodies and Supreme Audit Institutions to professionalise public-sector accounting across Africa.

FRCN
Financial Reporting Council of Nigeria

Statutory Nigerian body enforcing accounting, auditing, corporate-governance and financial-reporting standards. Ch1 §1.7

GAAFR
Government Accounting, Auditing and Financial Reporting

Known as "The Blue Book" — the US National Committee on Government Accounting's manual setting basic public-sector accounting principles.

SDGs
Sustainable Development Goals

The UN's global goals, referenced alongside AU Agenda 2063 as an aim PAFA works toward.

Ch 9 · Sources of Government Revenue

2 · Revenue collection & Federation Account bodies

See also the full Federation Account treatment and every numeric threshold in the other two guides — this list is the who's-who behind those numbers.

NRS
Nigeria Revenue Service

Formerly the FIRS (Federal Inland Revenue Service) — renamed under the 2025 tax-reform Acts. Collects Companies Income Tax, Petroleum Profits Tax, VAT, and the PAYE/Capital Gains Tax/Stamp Duties of Armed Forces, Police, Foreign Affairs and FCT staff.

NNPCL
NNPC Limited

Incorporated under CAMA per the Petroleum Industry Act 2021 — collects crude oil/gas sales revenue on the Federal Government's behalf.

NUPRC
Nigerian Upstream Petroleum Regulatory Commission

Regulates upstream petroleum operations; collects royalties, gas-flare penalties and signature bonuses. Absorbed the old DPR's upstream functions.

NMDPRA
Nigerian Midstream and Downstream Petroleum Regulatory Authority

Licenses and prices midstream/downstream operations and sets the Domestic Supply Obligation (DSO). Absorbed DPR's downstream side, PPPRA and the Petroleum Equalisation Fund.

DPR
Department of Petroleum Resources

The predecessor regulator, now split between NUPRC (upstream) and NMDPRA (downstream).

PPPRA
Petroleum Products Pricing Regulatory Agency

Predecessor pricing body, absorbed into NMDPRA.

PIA
Petroleum Industry Act, 2021

Restructured NNPC into NNPCL and created NUPRC and NMDPRA.

DSO
Domestic Supply Obligation

The quantity of petroleum products a producer must reserve for the domestic market — set by NMDPRA.

NCS
Nigeria Customs Service

Collects import, export and excise duties. Since the Customs Service Act 2023, funded by a 4% FOB (Free On Board) levy rather than a Federation Account share.

SBIRS
State Board of Internal Revenue Service

Collects state-level personal income tax outside the NRS categories — established under the Personal Income Tax Act 1993.

CIT
Companies Income Tax

Tax on the profits of registered companies, collected by NRS.

PPT
Petroleum Profits Tax

Tax on the profits of companies in petroleum operations (also referred to as Hydrocarbon Tax).

VAT
Value Added Tax

Consumption tax on goods and services, collected by NRS. See the 15/50/35 Federal/State/Local VAT pool split.

PAYE
Pay As You Earn

Personal income tax deducted at source. PAYE of Armed Forces, Police, Foreign Affairs and FCT staff is the one revenue type that bypasses the Federation Account and goes straight to the CRF.

CGT
Capital Gains Tax

Tax on gains from disposing of a chargeable asset.

FAAC
Federation Accounts Allocation Committee

Meets monthly in technical and plenary sessions to review inflows and allocate the Federation Account. Full treatment →

SJLGAAC
State Joint Local Government Account Allocation Committee

Historically distributed the local-government share; superseded for direct-to-council payment by the Supreme Court judgment of 11 July 2024. Full treatment →

RMAFC
Revenue Mobilisation, Allocation and Fiscal Commission

Reviews the revenue-allocation formula and monitors Federation Account accruals/disbursement. Full treatment →

JRB
Joint Revenue Board of Nigeria

2025 tax-reform body coordinating tax policy/administration across all three tiers. Full treatment →

Ch 10 · Public Procurement

3 · Procurement bodies & methods

NCPP
National Council on Public Procurement

Policy-making body chaired by the Minister of Finance, with the Attorney-General, SGF, Head of Service, Economic Adviser to the President and 6 part-time members.

BPP
Bureau of Public Procurement

Regulatory/supervisory body, headed by a Director-General — harmonises procurement policy and ensures probity, accountability and transparency.

NCB
National Competitive Bidding

A procurement method for mid-value contracts. See the exact value thresholds.

ICB
International Competitive Bidding

The procurement method for the highest-value contracts, open to international bidders.

RFQ
Request for Quotations

The low-value procurement method — at least 3 written quotations from unrelated suppliers.

EOI
Expression of Interest

Submitted by prospective bidders at the pre-qualification stage of a procurement process.

QCBS
Quality- and Cost-Based Selection

A standard consultancy-procurement selection method weighing technical quality against price. (Not spelt out in the chapter's own prose — a standard procurement term used there only in a threshold table; verify the exact local usage with your tutor.)

Ch 2, 7, 13, 18, 23 · Treasury, warrants, vouchers & audit

4 · Treasury, warrants & audit

AGF
Accountant-General of the Federation

Head of the Treasury, and issuer/custodian of every warrant on the CRF.

OAGF
Office of the Accountant-General of the Federation

Houses the Treasury; developed the ATRRS accounting system.

CAGD
Controller and Accountant-General's Department

Ghana's equivalent of the AGF's office — issues warrants directly to cost centres based on MOFEP's monthly warrants. Ch7 §7.11

PAC
Public Accounts Committee

Legislative committee that studies the Auditor-General's reports and exposes fraud, waste and inefficiency.

A-in-A
Appropriation-in-Aid

Income a department earns itself (fees, sales, rent) that it may apply directly against its own expenditure rather than remitting wholly to the CRF.

PGW
Provisional General Warrant

Issued before the Appropriation Act, capped at the previous year's level, max 6 months. Full warrant system →

AGW
Annual General Warrant

The main post-Act release for personal emoluments and services in the approved budget.

SGW
Supplementary General Warrant

Releases provision made in the approved supplementary estimates.

REW
Reserve Expenditure Warrant

Releases items the Minister of Finance deliberately withheld from the AGW/SGW for special control.

VW
Virement Warrant

Moves savings between sub-heads of the same head — cannot create a new sub-head or raise the total appropriated.

DFAGW / DFREW / DFSGW
Development Fund Annual / Reserve-Expenditure / Supplementary General Warrant

The Development Fund's own equivalents of AGW/REW/SGW, for capital rather than recurrent expenditure.

DFSW
Development Fund Supplementary Warrant or Development Fund Special Warrant

See §13 below — the source text uses this one abbreviation for two different warrants. Ch7

FTeR
Federal Treasury e-Receipt

Mandatory digital-receipt system for tax/levy/revenue payments; issues a Unique Reference Number (URN) per transaction.

URN
Unique Reference Number

Issued per transaction under FTeR.

PERC
Personal Emoluments Record Card

Document opened at the start of every financial year for each serving officer, recording their salary/allowance movements.

SRV / SIV
Store Receipt Voucher / Store Issue Voucher

Evidence receipt into, and authorise issue from, a government store. Full document glossary →

Ch 21 · Emerging Issues in the Nigerian Public Sector

5 · Digital / e-government reform systems

TSA
Treasury Single Account

2004 PFM-reform bank-account structure giving a unified view of government cash and curbing idle MDA balances held outside central control.

GIFMIS
Government Integrated Financial Management Information System

IT-based budget-management/accounting system spanning MDAs — a sub-component of the ERGP.

ERGP
Economic Reform and Governance Project

Approved December 2004 — the programme GIFMIS and ATRRS both sit under.

ATRRS
Accounting Transaction Recording and Reporting System

ICT accounting software developed by the OAGF, a bridge toward full GIFMIS adoption.

IPPIS
Integrated Personnel and Payroll Information System

Centralises personnel records and payroll administration across MDAs.

MBS
Merchant Buyer Solution

The platform behind NRS's mandatory e-invoicing system.

APP
Access Point Provider

A licensed intermediary in the NRS e-invoicing (MBS) system.

TMRAS
Treasury Management and Revenue Assurance System

Integrated platform linking treasuries, revenue agencies and the CBN for real-time cash pooling and leakage-tracking.

PPP
Public-Private Partnership

Private-sector co-financing of infrastructure — a toll road or a passport-issuance contract, for example.

Independent Corrupt Practices and Other Related Offences Commission

BOFIA

KYC

Ch 4, 5 · Pensions and Gratuity · Employee Benefits

7 · Pension bodies

Fully explained, with every rate and threshold, in "PS by the numbers" §1 — this is just the name key.

PenCom
National Pension Commission

Regulates and supervises the Contributory Pension Scheme.

PFA
Pension Fund Administrator

Manages individual Retirement Savings Accounts (RSAs) on contributors' behalf.

PFC
Pension Fund Custodian

Holds pension assets in custody, separate from the PFA that manages them. See the collision warning in §13 — this abbreviation means something else entirely in Ch16's GFS sector classification.

RSA
Retirement Savings Account

The individual account each contributor holds with a PFA.

PTAD
Pension Transitional Arrangements Directorate

Administers the pensions of retirees still on the old (pre-2004) Defined Benefit Scheme.

RLA / PW
Retiree Life Annuity / Programmed Withdrawal

The two ways a Contributory Pension Scheme retiree may access their RSA balance.

Ch 2, 3, 6, 16 · Framework & classification chapters

8 · Classification & reporting terms

GGS
General Government Sector

All resident central/state/local government units, social security funds, and controlled non-market non-profit institutions (schools, hospitals, courts) — excludes GBEs. Ch16

GBE
Government Business Enterprise

A profit-oriented public entity reporting under IFRS rather than IPSAS — the counterpart to GGS.

PNFC
Public Non-Financial Corporation

A GFS institutional-sector classification for a non-financial, publicly-owned corporation.

GFS
Government Financial Statistics

The IMF manual/framework underlying the GGS/PNFC/PFC sector classifications.

NCOA
National Chart of Accounts

Standard budget/accounting classification code structure used across Nigerian public-sector budgeting. Ch3, Ch6

COFOG
Classification of the Functions of Government

A GFS/NCOA-aligned functional classification standard for government spending.

GPFR(s)
General Purpose Financial Report(s)

The umbrella term for every report a public-sector entity produces for users who cannot demand information to meet their own needs. Ch3

GPFS
General Purpose Financial Statements

One part of GPFRs specifically — every GPFS is a GPFR, but not every GPFR (a budget document, a service-performance report) is a GPFS. Ch3

MTEF
Medium-Term Expenditure Framework

Multi-year fiscal planning framework the annual budget must be derived from. Deadlines →

FSP
Fiscal Strategy Paper

One of the four components the MTEF must contain, alongside the macroeconomic framework, the expenditure/revenue framework and the consolidated debt statement.

10 · The Acts, and what each one creates

Every body in this guide exists because some Act created it. Knowing the pairing — which Act, which body — is a recurring "which Act established X" question in its own right.

The 1999 Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria (as amended)
Establishes the CRF, Development Fund, Contingency Fund, the offices of Accountant-General and Auditor-General, the Federation Account (s.162), the Code of Conduct Bureau (Third Schedule Part I), and the National Assembly/Federal Executive Council themselves.
Finance (Control and Management) Act, 1958
Creates the Development Fund (s.25), the warrant system, and the eight generic fund types (general, capital project, special, trust, contingency, inter-governmental service, revolving, self-liquidating) — see the fund-types guide.
Allocation of Revenue (Federation Account, etc.) Act
Establishes FAAC and governs how the Federation Account is shared between the three tiers.
Revenue Mobilisation, Allocation and Fiscal Commission Act, 1989
Establishes RMAFC.
Joint Revenue Board (Establishment) Act, 2025
Part of the 2025 tax reform — establishes the JRB and renames FIRS to NRS.
Fiscal Responsibility Act, 2007
Establishes the Fiscal Responsibility Commission (spelt out in full in the source — no standard abbreviation, to avoid confusion with FRCN). Mandates the MTEF, the 3%-of-GDP deficit ceiling, and the 80%-of-operating-surplus remittance rule. Numbers →
Public Procurement Act, 2007
Establishes the NCPP and the BPP.
Pension Reform Act, 2014
Establishes PenCom and the Contributory Pension Scheme (PFAs, PFCs, RSAs). Numbers →
Petroleum Industry Act, 2021
Restructures NNPC into NNPCL and creates NUPRC and NMDPRA, absorbing the old DPR and PPPRA.
Customs Service Act, 2023
Reforms NCS funding to a 4% FOB levy, replacing the old 7% cost-of-collection deduction.
Corrupt Practices and Other Related Offences Act, 2000
Establishes ICPC.
Economic and Financial Crimes Commission (Establishment) Act, 2004
Establishes EFCC.
Money Laundering (Prohibition) Act · Advance Fee Fraud Act · Failed Banks (Recovery of Debts) Act · BOFIA
The suite of Acts EFCC enforces.
Financial Reporting Council of Nigeria Act, 2011
Establishes FRCN.
Personal Income Tax Act, 1993
Basis for SBIRS collecting state-level personal income tax.
Companies and Allied Matters Act (CAMA), 2020
The general company-registration statute — governs NNPCL's own incorporation as a limited company.

Worth knowing · Same abbreviation, different things

13 · Same letters, different things

PFC means two unrelated things in this syllabus

In Ch4/Ch5 (pensions), PFC is the Pension Fund Custodian — the body that holds pension assets in custody. In Ch16 (GFS sector classification), PFC is a Public Financial Corporation — an entirely different institutional-sector category. The two never appear in the same question, but if a past paper's option list ever looks like it's testing "PFC" out of context, check which chapter the question is actually drawn from before answering.

DFSW means two different warrants in the source itself

Chapter 7's own text abbreviates both the "Development Fund Supplementary Warrant" and the "Development Fund Special Warrant" as DFSW — the ambiguity is in the source, not an error introduced here. Always refer to a Development Fund warrant by its full name rather than the abbreviation.

"FRC" is deliberately never used for the Fiscal Responsibility Commission

The Fiscal Responsibility Act, 2007 establishes the Fiscal Responsibility Commission — the source text never abbreviates it, and this guide follows suit, precisely because "FRC" would be one letter away from FRCN (Financial Reporting Council of Nigeria), a completely different body from a completely different Act. Write it in full.
